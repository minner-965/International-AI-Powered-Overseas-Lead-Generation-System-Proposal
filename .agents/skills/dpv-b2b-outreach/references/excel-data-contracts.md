This skill does not parse or create business imports. When drafting copy from imported data, consume only canonical records produced after dry-run, human approval, and commit.

Never use formulas, macros, external links, raw row payloads, source or staging paths, supplier cost, historical customer price, private notes, or ambiguous/unknown taxonomy values as outreach facts. Product references must retain real `product_master.id` values.
