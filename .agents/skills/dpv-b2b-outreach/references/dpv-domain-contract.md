# DPV domain contract

The following facts are inputs and remain read-only: company identity, verification, lifecycle, exclusion and relationship status; Buyer Business Model; Category Procurement Match; Product Opportunity and `product_master` foreign keys; Supplier Access; Product Access Matrix; Readiness; decision-maker and contact verification; DPV Score; management and Mexico historical references; suppression and CRM history; taxonomy, catalog snapshots and evidence IDs.

Copy quality, generic prospecting scores, reply text, or contact verification must never upgrade these facts or compensate for a missing Product Match. A reply may create a sales task or a draft for review; it may not rewrite qualification.
