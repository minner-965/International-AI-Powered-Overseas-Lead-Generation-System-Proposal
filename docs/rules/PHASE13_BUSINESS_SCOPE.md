# Phase 13 business scope

Authority: Phase 13 V2.1 plan, MC03 (2026-09-08). Status: proposed rules for read-only comparison; not applied to production.

DPV evaluates category-level supply cooperation in approved womenswear and general-merchandise scope. Use the database's current approved category/profile scope and traceable company-side evidence. Imported products and historical transactions inform scope/ICP, not a requirement for an exact SKU. A company operating a matching category can have a business opportunity without an explicit current procurement notice.

Evaluate all current companies, including large companies, small businesses and records without a current opportunity projection. Do not invent category observations, identity verification or type evidence from a company name. Distinguish missing evidence from explicit negative evidence. Preserve source URLs, evidence payloads and all historical decisions.

Business fit, contact readiness and email eligibility are independent dimensions. Missing buyer, email, telephone, WhatsApp, import record or supplier portal does not alone exclude a business opportunity. Existing customer, suppression and previous outreach remain separate governance facts.

MC03 reads existing database evidence only: no search/Hunter calls, no SMTP, no database mutation, no production rule activation. MC04 requires a separate approval after review of MC03 results.
